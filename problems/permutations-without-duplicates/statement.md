Write a method to compute all permutations of a string of unique characters.

## Example 1:

**Input:** s = "abc"  
**Output:** ["abc","acb","bac","bca","cab","cba"]

## Example 2:

**Input:** s = "ab"  
**Output:** ["ab","ba"]

## Example 3:

**Input:** s = "a"  
**Output:** ["a"]

## Constraints:

- `1 <= s.length <= 6`
- `s` consists of unique lowercase English letters
